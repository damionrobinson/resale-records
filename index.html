The problem

Two different years, four platforms, one deadline

  • Platforms report by calendar year

    Each platform tells HMRC about you if you make 30 or more sales, or take more than €2,000 (about £1,700), on that platform in a calendar year.

  • Tax works on the tax year

    The £1,000 trading allowance and Self Assessment run from 6 April to 5 April. Your platform totals won't line up with that unless you re-sort them.

  • Not every sale is trading

    Selling your own old things is usually different from buying to resell. You need a record showing which was which.

    Questions

    Common questions

    Does being reported mean I owe tax?

    No. A platform report tells HMRC what you sold. Whether you owe anything depends on whether you were trading and your profit. GOV.UK has a checker for additional income, and explains the platform reporting rules.

    Is this tax advice?

    No. Resale Records organises your records and shows the published rules. It won't tell you whether you're trading or what you owe. If you're unsure, use HMRC's guidance or an accountant.

    Do you connect to my accounts?

    No. You download your own sales files from each platform and upload them. We never ask for your platform passwords.

    What happens to my data?

    Your sales stay in your own browser on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us. Read the privacy notice.
