Paste from Vinted

  1. On Vinted (on a computer), open Balance › Payment history.
  2. Pick a month, press Cmd+A (Windows: Ctrl+A) and copy.
  3. Paste below. You can paste several months, one after another; repeats are skipped.

Only lines starting with "Sold" are imported. Withdrawals and purchases are ignored.
